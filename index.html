Accessibility

Built so everyone can read The Horizon

  • Listen to any article. Every article has a narrated audio edition, with slow, normal and fast speeds.
  • Change how the page looks. The Accessibility button (Aa) at the top sets larger text, relaxed spacing, an easy-read font, light or dark colors, and high contrast.
  • Calm mode turns off motion, decorations and bright colors for readers who find busy pages overwhelming.
  • Reading guide adds a highlight bar that follows your pointer, which helps some dyslexic readers keep their place.
  • Videos will have captions and a written transcript.
  • Keyboard and screen readers. Every control works with a keyboard, and a “Skip to content” link appears when you press Tab.

Something hard to use? Tell us on the Contact section below and we'll fix it.
